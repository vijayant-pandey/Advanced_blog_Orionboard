About Advanced Blog

We’re dedicated to building a powerful analytics-driven blog platform

Advanced Blog is a modern platform for authors, editors, and readers. It combines production-grade features like RBAC, moderation, analytics, and a rich writing experience with a beautiful magazine-style UI.

With detailed statistics (views, likes, comments, self vs. external views), authors can understand how their content performs and how far it reaches.

{# ===== Why choose us section ===== #}

Why Choose Advanced Blog

Whether you are an individual writer, a small publication, or a bigger editorial team, Advanced Blog gives you tools for:

  • * Role-based access control (Admin / Author / Editor / Reader)
  • * Detailed views, likes, and comments analytics
  • * Moderated comments with nesting and approval workflow
  • * Per-post edit tracking and revision-friendly design
  • * Geo-based reach stats (country/city for views)
  • * API-first design ready for React/mobile apps
